## About

Ad Astra takes Minecraft to space. Build a launchpad, assemble a tiered rocket, fit a space suit with an oxygen tank, and fly to the Moon, Mars, Venus, Mercury or Glacio. Each body has its own gravity, atmosphere, hazards and resources — and rovers to cross the surface once you land.

---

## Features

- Five destinations: Moon, Mars, Venus, Mercury and Glacio, each with real gravity differences
- Tiered rockets built at a launchpad, each tier reaching further
- Space suits with oxygen tanks, and oxygen distribution for pressurised bases
- Rovers and space vehicles for surface travel
- Full machine chain: compressor, fuel refinery, oxygen loader and power generation

## Installation

1. **Download** the JAR matching your Minecraft version from the table above.
2. **Install a mod loader** — Forge, Fabric or NeoForge for your exact game version.
3. **Place the `.jar`** into your `.minecraft/mods/` folder (create it if it is missing).
4. **Launch** Minecraft using the modded profile.
5. Verify **Ad Astra** appears in the in-game mods list.

> **Tip:** if the game crashes on startup, you almost certainly mixed a Legacy JAR with a Modern
> Minecraft version, or are missing a required dependency. Check `latest.log` first.

## FAQ

**Q: Do I need oxygen everywhere?**
A: Yes on airless bodies. A space suit with a filled tank, or an oxygen-distributed room, is mandatory.

**Q: How do I get back?**
A: Your landed rocket can be re-fuelled and launched again — bring fuel or build production on site.

**Q: Does low gravity change combat?**
A: It changes jumping and fall damage significantly, which matters more than you expect.

**Q: Is it safe to add to an existing world?**
A: Yes in most cases — back the save up first, as you should with any mod addition.

**Q: Can I use it in a public modpack?**
A: Yes. Both builds are modpack-ready and require no additional setup.
